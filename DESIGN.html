Our model

A weekly rhythm.

We aggregate orders, run a single weekly route through the villages, do quality checks at pickup, and pay farmers within 7 days. Simple, predictable, fair.
01 / MONDAY

You order

Browse rice by variety, village, or farmer. Order any pack from 1kg to 50kg. Cut-off is Tuesday 9pm.

02 / WEDNESDAY

Farmer confirms

Your farmer gets a WhatsApp message with the order. They mill the rice fresh that week and prepare your sack.

03 / FRIDAY

We collect

Our team runs a single weekly tempo route through all eight villages. Each sack is weighed and quality-checked at pickup.

04 / SATURDAY

You receive

Delivery to your door or a city pickup point. Farmer is paid via UPI by Sunday evening — no waiting weeks.

The fair-price math.

We take a 10% commission. Farmers still earn 2× what mandi pays. You pay 30-40% less than branded retail.
